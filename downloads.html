Android Sideloading Instructions

  1. Download the ShareLink.apk package from the official GitHub Releases link above.
  2. If prompted by Android, permit installation from your browser or file manager ("Install Unknown Apps").
  3. Open ShareLink and grant runtime permissions for Camera (for QR scanning) and Nearby Devices (for local Wi-Fi discovery).
  4. You are ready to send and receive files completely offline!

Connecting From Windows, iPhone, or Mac (No App Needed)

You do not need to install an app on Windows, Mac, or iOS to receive or send files with ShareLink:

  • Step 1: Connect your computer or iPhone to the same Wi-Fi network or Android portable hotspot.
  • Step 2: Scan the QR code displayed on the Android sender screen with your phone camera or enter the displayed local link (e.g., http://192.168.43.1:8080/s/...#pairingSecret) into Chrome, Edge, or Safari.
  • Step 3: The ShareLink Web Receiver opens directly in your browser. All decryption occurs locally in the browser using the Web Crypto API.
