Frictionless Workflow

How ShareLink Works

Transfer files of any size in three secure, simple steps without an internet connection.

01

Select Files on Android

Open the ShareLink app on your Android device. Select any combination of photos, videos, apps (APK), PDFs, or documents with instant deferred isolate hashing.

02

Scan or Share QR Code

ShareLink launches a secure local server and displays a pairing QR code. The pairing secret is encoded in a URL hash fragment (#secret), ensuring it is never sent across the network wire.

03

Transfer at Wire Speed

The receiving device—whether another Android phone, a Windows laptop, or an iPhone—connects over Wi-Fi or hotspot. Files stream directly between devices with AEAD encryption and verified SHA-256 integrity.

Engineered For Performance

Verified Core Capabilities

Every feature listed below is implemented and tested in the ShareLink engineering core.

SLTP v2 & AES-256-GCM

Our custom ShareLink Transfer Protocol (SLTP v2) packages 512 KB chunks into deterministic 40-byte binary framed packets with 128-bit authentication tags to prevent tampering.

Pure Offline Operation

Works completely without cellular data or internet access. Connect through an existing Wi-Fi router or turn on your Android portable hotspot for anywhere sharing.

Zero-Install Web Receiver

Receiving on Windows, Mac, or iOS? No app installation required! Simply scan the QR code with your camera to open the high-performance local web receiver directly in your browser.

Large Files & Resumable Streams

Transfer 10 GB+ videos without memory crashes. Memory-bounded streaming with backpressure flushing and RFC 7233 range resumption recovers paused transfers seamlessly.

Zero-Knowledge QR Pairing

Transfer session secrets are encoded exclusively in the URL hash fragment (#pairingSecret), ensuring symmetric encryption keys are derived client-side and never transit the wire.

Automatic Storage & Media Indexing

Files are saved safely into public Downloads/ShareLink. MediaScanner is automatically invoked so photos and videos immediately appear in your device's Gallery.
